Privacy notice

Draft — requires legal review

This is a structural draft written to establish what this page must cover and in what register. It is not a legal document, it has not been reviewed by counsel, and it must not be published as drafted. §32A treats the privacy notice, terms and cookie policy as requiring professional review before launch.

Note in particular that SP Gems accepts enquiries from the EU and UK, which brings GDPR obligations regardless of where the company is registered.

What we collect

Only what you give us. When you submit an enquiry, a consultation request, a commission enquiry or a trade brief, we collect the details you enter on that form: your name, email address, country, any phone or messaging handle you choose to give, your message, and the reference of any stone or piece the enquiry relates to.

We do not collect payment details through this website. There is no checkout.

Why we ask for your country

Because export documentation and compliance review depend on it. It is a required field for that reason and not for marketing segmentation.

What we do with it

We use it to answer your enquiry and, if a transaction follows, to prepare the documentation for it. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Analytics

To be completed once the analytics choice is made. §24 requires an analytics and event layer; §25 and §32A require that whatever is chosen is disclosed here accurately, with a working consent mechanism where consent is required.

How long we keep it

Needs confirmation. A stated retention period is required, distinguishing between an enquiry that went nowhere and transaction records SP Gems may be obliged to retain.

Your rights

Needs legal input. Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection, with a named contact route and a stated response time.

Uploaded files

Images uploaded to a commission enquiry are stored privately, never at a guessable address, and are stripped of EXIF metadata on arrival — photographs from phones routinely carry GPS coordinates, and we do not want them.
