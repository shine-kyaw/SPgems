A structural draft only, written to establish scope. Not reviewed by counsel and not to be published as drafted.

Nothing here is an offer to sell

This website is a catalogue and a reference. Listings, including prices and price bands, are indicative and are not an offer capable of acceptance. A sale arises only from a written agreement between SP Gems and a buyer, after enquiry and review.

This is not a technicality. Fine coloured stones are priced against a moving market, and every transaction is subject to a compliance review of the destination jurisdiction before any commitment is made.

Availability

Availability shown on a stone is our best current record and is verified periodically. A stone marked available may have been reserved between your reading the page and our reading your enquiry. Where that happens we will say so immediately.

Descriptions and imagery

We photograph stones on a neutral mid-grey ground with a colour reference target, and we do not saturate them. Even so, no screen reproduces a gemstone exactly, and stones behave differently in different light. This is why we offer to show you a stone on a video call before you commit to anything.

Laboratory report numbers are published so you can verify them at the laboratory. A report is that laboratory's finding, not a warranty from us.

Intellectual property

Photography, text and the design of this site are ours. Public image derivatives are watermarked and served below full resolution; full-resolution files are released on enquiry. Image theft is endemic in this trade and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.
