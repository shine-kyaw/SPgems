Since 1985

Our story

Most luxury brands invent their heritage. Ours is checkable, which is a different kind of asset and worth stating plainly rather than decorating.

1985 — the trade

The SanPya family began trading precious gems in Mogok in 1985. That is a family business in the ordinary sense: buying rough from miners in the valley, sorting it, and selling it on. It is the least glamorous part of this trade and the part where the expertise actually accumulates, because a sorter who is wrong loses money the same week.

2007 — the company

SP Gems was formally established in 2007, after two decades of trading. Incorporation changed what the business could do: hold licences, employ cutters, invest in its own workings, and deal directly with buyers outside Myanmar rather than through the layers that usually sit between a Mogok sorter and a collector in Geneva.

Now — mine to jewel

The business today spans four things: workings we operate in the Mogok valley; sourcing relationships with independent miners in Mogok and in Namyarseik, Kachin State; a cutting workshop in Yangon handling the full range from commercial goods to exceptional single stones; and a jewellery collection, The Value, headed by Aung Kyaw Zin.

That span is the whole commercial argument for this house. A dealer in Bangkok can match our stones. They cannot tell you which hole in the ground a stone came out of, because they were not there and neither was the person who sold it to them.

What we are not going to tell you

Our previous website said our clients included “some of the most recognized world brand, prominent celebrities and public figures.” We have removed that sentence. We cannot name a single one of them with their permission, and an unsupported claim of that kind is read by any sophisticated buyer as evidence that the claim is soft — which makes it worse than saying nothing.

The same applies to the ethics language that page carried. What we can document is on the sourcing page, in the form of records rather than adjectives.
