Editorial position

We answer the question, including when the honest answer does not help us sell

The most-read thing we will ever publish is an explanation of heat treatment. There is a version of that article which implies every heated stone is compromised and every unheated stone is precious. It would be commercially convenient and it would be wrong, and a knowledgeable reader can tell within two paragraphs.

So we write the true version. Heating is legitimate, long-established, and often desirable. Unheated material of comparable quality is rarer and costs more. Both statements are true at once, and a buyer who understands why is a better buyer to deal with.

Migration note

The existing site's 2015–2017 posts are almost entirely duplicated gallery posts: nine CSR posts sharing one headline and one date, three near-identical 2017 sale posts, five near-identical 2015 gala posts. They are a live duplicate-content liability.

The recommendation is not to migrate them as posts. Extract the photography, consolidate into two or three properly written retrospective stories, and 301-redirect the old URLs to those.
