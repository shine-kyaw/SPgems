Remaining articles in the launch set

Twelve articles at launch, each 1,200–2,000 words, every one illustrated with photographs of our own stones and cross-linked to live inventory. Depth is the ranking mechanism in this category, and stock photography of generic gemstones is prohibited.

Needs confirmation from SP Gems

Every article should carry a byline from a named person with stated credentials. Anonymous authority is not authority, and for this category it is the difference between a reader trusting the explanation and skimming it.

Required: who will be the named expert voice, and what gemmological credentials can be stated — GIA GG, FGA, AIGS, or similar held by anyone in the business. If such a credential exists it is a significant asset and is currently unstated anywhere.
