Reading the catalogue

What the fields mean, and what we deliberately do not publish

Treatment is stated on every stone, always. Most rubies and sapphires on the market are heated; that is accepted practice and not a defect. What matters is that it is disclosed, and that the disclosure is as prominent for treated stones as for untreated ones. That is what makes it credible.

Origin distinguishes between a region a laboratory has confirmed and one that is our own assessment. We never blur the two. Mogok is not a synonym for Burma and carries a different price.

Clarity is described rather than graded. Coloured stones are not assessed on the diamond clarity scale, and applying it to them signals inexperience.

Price is shown as a band on most stones so you can tell whether we are in your range before you write to us, and on request for exceptional material, where the market moves. Per-carat pricing is a trade metric and is released to trade accounts only. The full certificate scan is released on enquiry: the report number is public because you can verify it at the laboratory, but the scan itself is the asset most often copied.
