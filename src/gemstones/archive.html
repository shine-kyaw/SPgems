Archive

Stones that have sold. We keep every record permanently, at its original address, with all specifications and photography intact.

Why we keep these

A young digital presence has one persuasive asset available to it: evidence of what has actually passed through the business. Deleting sold stones throws that away, along with whatever search authority those pages have accumulated.

So the records stay, with the price and the enquiry button removed and a “sold” state in their place. If something here is close to what you are looking for, tell us — a specific brief is usually easier for us to fill than a browse.
