The top tier

Exceptional Gems

A handful of stones at any one time. These are the pieces we would not put in a three-column grid: material where the size, the saturation, the absence of treatment and the confirmed origin arrive together, which happens rarely and not to a schedule.

How this tier works

Specifications, dimensions, treatment status and laboratory report numbers are published in full, exactly as they are on every other stone. Price is on request only — not as a device, but because material at this level is priced against a market that moves, and a fixed public number would be wrong within the quarter.

Enquiries from this tier are flagged for priority response, and a private viewing can be arranged in Yangon or by video call before any commitment.
