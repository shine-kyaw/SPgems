Cookies & local storage

This demo build sets no cookies at all

Worth stating precisely, because “we value your privacy” banners have made the word meaningless. This build sets no cookies. It does use two browser storage mechanisms, both of which stay on your own device and are never transmitted anywhere:

  • localStorage. Three preferences: whether you had the advanced filter panel open, whether you prefer the catalogue in one column or two, and nothing else.
  • sessionStorage. Partly completed multi-step forms, so that going back in your browser does not lose what you typed; and whether you dismissed the demo notice at the top of the page. Cleared when you close the tab.

Both are readable and clearable through your browser's developer tools. Neither is used to identify or track you.

Fonts

This demo loads two typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to a Google server. In the production build the fonts are self-hosted and subset, so that request does not happen — both for privacy and because self-hosted, preloaded fonts are faster.

Analytics

None in this build. Once an analytics tool is chosen it must be documented here accurately, with a working consent mechanism where consent is required, and third-party scripts kept to a hard minimum — each one is a performance and a privacy cost.
