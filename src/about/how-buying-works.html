How buying works

There is no cart on this site, so the process is worth setting out properly. Nine steps, including the ones that are slow and the ones that involve paperwork. If any of this looks like more friction than you want, that is useful information before you start rather than after.

  1. You enquire

    From a stone's page, a piece's page, or the contact page. The enquiry carries the stone reference automatically, so we know exactly what you are asking about without you having to describe it. You can also reach us on WhatsApp, Viber or email — though those lose the reference, so please quote it.

  2. A specialist replies

    A person, not an autoresponder, normally within one business day. Note that we are on Myanmar Standard Time, which is UTC+06:30 — a half-hour offset that surprises people. If you are in Europe or the Americas, expect the reply overnight.

    Response commitment to be confirmed by SP Gems before publication.

  3. We send the full file

    Additional images at full resolution, video where it exists, and the complete laboratory report as a scan. The report number is already public on the stone's page so you can verify it at the laboratory independently, before you speak to us at all.

  4. You see the stone, if you want to

    By video call — the stone in hand, under daylight and incandescent light, moved under the light so you can see how it behaves rather than how it photographs. Or in person in Yangon, by appointment.

  5. Price and terms are agreed

    Prices on this site are bands, or on request for exceptional material. The final number is agreed in conversation. That is not a device: fine coloured stones are priced against a market that moves, and a fixed public figure is frequently wrong by the quarter.

  6. Documentation and compliance review

    Before any commitment, a human reviews the transaction against the destination country. This is why we ask for your country on the enquiry form and why we do not operate an unattended checkout. We assemble the documentation set: purchase records, the laboratory report, and export paperwork.

    Requires legal input. See international purchasing.

  7. Payment

    Needs confirmation from SP Gems. What methods are actually available to a Myanmar entity receiving international payment, and what deposit terms apply. Buyers ask this early and a vague answer costs the sale.

  8. Insured shipping

    Needs confirmation from SP Gems. Which carrier, what insurance cover, which countries are served, typical transit time, and who bears duty and import tax.

  9. After the sale

    Resizing, resetting, care advice, and re-certification if you want a different laboratory's opinion later. A stone bought here stays on this site permanently in the archive, at its original address, with its full record intact.

Why three steps above are unanswered

Payment, shipping and the compliance detail cannot be written by an agency. They depend on facts only SP Gems holds: what banking routes are actually available, which carrier and insurance arrangement is in place, and what counsel advises about which markets can be served on what documentary basis.

Inventing plausible answers here would be the single most damaging thing this website could do. An international buyer who is told one thing on the website and another in an email has learned something about the company, and it is not good.
