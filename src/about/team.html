Design

Aung Kyaw Zin

Gem designer. Heads The Value.

Took an architecture degree in 2003 before turning to gem design, and that training shows in the work: settings resolved in section as well as in plan, and galleries designed as carefully as the face of a piece.

The Value is his collection. Pieces are built around specific stones rather than designed first and filled afterwards, which is slower and produces one-of-a-kind work rather than a line.

For each person: name, role, a short biography, a photograph, and any gemmological credential held — GIA GG, FGA, AIGS or similar. If anyone in the business holds one, it is a significant and currently unstated asset, and it should appear here and in the byline on every education article.

Also worth naming: which languages your team works in. An international buyer choosing between two houses will pick the one whose website tells them someone speaks their language.
