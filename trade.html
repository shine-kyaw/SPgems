Trade & wholesale

Buying for the trade

If you are a designer, a brand's sourcing lead, a bench jeweller or a dealer, this page is for you rather than the rest of the site. Different questions, different answers.

Capability

What we can actually do

We mine, we sort, and we cut. For a trade buyer that means three things a reseller cannot offer: depth in a given material rather than one showpiece, the ability to cut to a brief rather than sell you what exists, and documentation that goes back further than the last invoice.

It also means we are honest about range. We cut across the full spectrum from commercial goods to exceptional single stones, and if what you need is calibrated material in volume we will tell you whether we are the right supplier rather than take the order and disappoint you.

What we offer the trade

  • Matched pairs and suites. Cut from a single piece of rough where possible, which is why our matches are closer than pairs assembled from a parcel. Dimensions matched to within 0.05–0.06 mm across all three axes.
  • Cutting to specification. Given a brief — dimensions, proportions, a particular face-up size — we can cut to it from rough rather than hunt for something close.
  • Sourcing briefs. Tell us the material, carat range, treatment status and budget and we will work the brief. We are in Mogok every month, and a specific brief is often easier for us to fill than a browse.
  • Chain-of-custody documentation. Purchase records, laboratory reports and export paperwork, per stone, for your own client's compliance team. The position on that is here.

Trade accounts

A trade account unlocks per-carat pricing, full-resolution imagery, certificate documents, parcel and lot listings, and a downloadable inventory export. It is a Phase 2 build, for a specific reason: it requires SP Gems to define a trade pricing policy and approval criteria, and publishing an application form for something that does not yet exist behind it would waste your time and ours.

In the meantime, tell us who you are and what you need, and we will deal with it directly.

This page currently claims four capabilities: matched pairs, cutting to specification, parcels and lots, and calibrated goods. Each needs confirming before publication — particularly calibrated goods, which is a genuinely different operation from single-stone cutting and which many source-side houses do not do.

Also required: trade terms, minimum order values if any, lead times, and whether memo or consignment is available. Persona 4 asks all of these in their first email.
