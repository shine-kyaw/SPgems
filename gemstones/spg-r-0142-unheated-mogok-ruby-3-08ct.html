Provenance

Where this stone came from

Recovered from our own workings in the Mogok valley and cut in our Yangon workshop. The rough was sorted in-house; the cutter took a deliberately shallow crown to hold colour through the table rather than chase weight.

The cut

Cut in our own workshop

This stone was cut in our Yangon workshop, from rough we sorted ourselves. Cutting our own material means the decision about how to treat a piece of rough — whether to hold weight or hold colour — is made by people who will also be answering for the finished stone.
