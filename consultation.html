Why a request, not a calendar

We would rather confirm a time than offer one we cannot keep

A live self-service booking calendar looks better than this form. It also requires an accurately maintained staff calendar, a defined availability policy, and reliable connectivity — and a booking system that offers slots nobody attends is far worse than an honest request.

So for now: you tell us when suits, we confirm within a business day. Live booking with automatic calendar invitations is a Phase 2 upgrade, once the availability practice behind it actually exists.

Needs confirmation from SP Gems

Business hours, working days, and public-holiday closures, so the form can flag a requested window that falls outside them — as an advisory, not a block. Also: whether visitors can be received at the Yangon office, and on what terms.
